Q: Which salesperson has the highest conversion rate this month?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
tool=text-to-sql  intent=read

Aarav Sharma has the highest conversion rate this month.

- **Salesperson:** **Aarav Sharma**  
- **Total opportunities created:** **2**  
- **Opportunities won:** **1**  
- **Conversion rate:** **50.0 %** (this month)

**Insight:** A 50 % conversion rate shows strong effectiveness for Aarav; the team could review his approach to replicate success across other reps.  

Would you like to see the conversion rates for all salespeople this month?

SQL: SELECT s.name AS salesperson,
       COUNT(o.id) AS total_opportunities,
       SUM(CASE WHEN o.stage = 'Closed Won' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS won_opportunities,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN o.stage = 'Closed Won' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / NULLIF(COUNT(o.id), 0), 2) AS conversion_rate_percent
FROM opportunities o
JOIN salespeople s ON o.owner_id = s.id
WHERE strftime('%Y-%m', o.created_at) = strftime('%Y-%m', 'now')
GROUP BY s.id
ORDER BY conversion_rate_percent DESC
LIMIT 1
rows: 1
